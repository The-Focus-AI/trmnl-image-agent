# Rotating Single-Focus Poster Archetypes Over AI Images or Dense Multi-Widget Pages

Instead of generating full-frame AI illustrations (which produced repetitive layouts and muddy 1-bit text) or cramming all feeds into micro-print on a single 130-DPI 800×480 screen, the display rotates every 15 minutes across 5 hand-crafted, single-focus HTML/CSS/SVG poster Archetypes (`dispatch` Sky & River, `broadsheet` Town Bulletin, `handbill` Coming Up Events, `almanac` Celestial & Seasonal Woods, and `poster` Special Advisory) built with a 14px–76px e-ink type scale and sturdy >=2.5px strokes. Decisions API chooses the lead poster for the hour and the calendar-gated Seasonal Spotlight while subsequent 15-minute slots cycle through the remaining posters, giving customers across-the-counter readability (~35–50 words per screen) alongside complete local coverage over time.
