# Cornwall Storefront Edition

A live e-ink publication displayed publicly in the store, giving neighbors and visitors an always-current read on local weather, town life, seasonal conditions, and notable news.

## Language

**Edition**:
A single published page shown on the store display for a given moment, uniting the day's lead angle, supporting items, and visual framing.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, screenshot, image

**Daypart**:
One of the distinct publishing windows of the day—Morning, Midday, or Evening/Tomorrow (plus Weekend)—each carrying its own editorial angle and preferred Archetypes.
_Avoid_: Cron cycle, refresh tick

**Archetype**:
A distinct print format—such as a village broadsheet, general-store handbill, almanac page, trail dispatch, or alert poster—selected to match the character and urgency of an Edition.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, style of the week, wireframe

**Town Dispatch**:
Storefront-appropriate local reporting, civic briefs, and upcoming community events drawn from Cornwall, Region 1, and Northwest Corner sources—excluding national politics, grim crime, and private household chores.
_Avoid_: News feed, generic headlines, reminders

**Seasonal Window**:
The strict calendar date span during which a local seasonal phenomenon (such as maple sugaring, skiing, river flow, farmers markets, or frost watch) is eligible to be considered for an Edition.
_Avoid_: Operational regime, backdrop season, season mode

**Seasonal Spotlight**:
A bounded feature slot within an Edition highlighting the single most notable in-window seasonal condition today without displacing weather or Town Dispatches.
_Avoid_: Primary story, active stack

**Almanac**:
Year-round astronomical and natural-history context for Cornwall—sunrise/sunset, daily daylight delta, moon phase, and seasonal wildlife or flora observations.
_Avoid_: Sun/moon widget, filler
